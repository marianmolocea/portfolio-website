Palette preview

Three colour directions for the one-page site, each shown as a small version of the real page with the real copy. Move the mouse over a panel to see the background react. Only the colours and the stand-in font change between panels.

Fonts are named per palette; this preview uses fonts already on your computer so it makes no network requests. Every ratio below was measured with a WCAG 2.2 contrast script. "Over canvas" means against the brightest dot the background can draw.

1 · Terminal Mint dark · black + mint

A green-tinted near-black with a soft mint accent: technical and calm, like a terminal that has been tidied up. Font direction: Space Grotesk on its own, 700 for headings and 400 for body.

  • background#0E1110
  • surface#161B19 · text 14.6:1
  • text#E6ECE9 · 15.9:1
  • muted#9BA6A1 · 7.6:1
  • accent (also link text)#6EE7B7 · 12.5:1
  • line#2A302D · decorative

Links vs body text 1.3:1, so links are underlined. Over canvas: body 10.5:1, muted 5.0:1. Dark text on a mint fill: 12.5:1.
